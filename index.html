01 / ABOUT THE STUDIO

Who we are

Rooted in heritage. Designed for today.

Baeroh is an interior design studio based in Jaipur.

Half of what we know came from Italy, where we learned to see a space long before it exists. The other half came from home, where we learned that seeing is the easy part.

A drawing is only a promise. We stay on site until it is kept.

We would rather a space be unmistakably yours than unmistakably ours. We know the work is done when someone stands inside it and feels more understood than when they walked in.

02 / WHY BAEROH?

Why Baeroh?

The word we grew up with.

Some names are invented. Ours was already being spoken.

Baero hai. Do you know? I know. In Rajasthan it is how something worth passing on gets passed on, because nobody knows everything and everybody knows something.

We pressed the two words together and kept them.

You know the life. We know the making.

Everything we do happens between the question and the answer.
